Designed for quick reuse, not clutter.

ClipNest is meant to feel like a quiet utility: always there when you need it, out of the way when you do not, and clear about where your data lives.

Searchable clipboard history

Find copied text quickly instead of recopying it. ClipNest keeps a browsable history you can filter from the main window.

Pins and safer cleanup

Pin important items, clear the rest, and undo recent deletes so cleanup is less risky during real work.

Rich text and images

Preview HTML and images, copy plain text when needed, and save image entries back out to files.

Optional Google sync

Sign in with Google if you want eligible clipboard items to sync between ClipNest installs on your devices.

Local-only when that is enough

You can use ClipNest entirely without signing in. Google sync is optional, not required for normal use.

Windows-native utility

Run at startup, access the tray menu, and keep clipboard history close without needing a browser tab or web dashboard.

How Google sync works

ClipNest stays local-first. When sync is enabled, it mirrors eligible clipboard history into your Google Drive appDataFolder, then other ClipNest installs signed into the same Google account can pull down those updates.

1. Capture locally

ClipNest saves clipboard history to your Windows device first so your local workflow stays fast.

→
2. Sync eligible items

Plain text, rich text, pinned state, and synced image files are written to your Google Drive app data folder.

What currently syncs

  • plain text
  • rich text / HTML
  • pinned state
  • image files used by synced entries

What may stay local-only

  • copied file lists
  • device-specific file paths
  • items you choose not to sync by remaining signed out

Built for privacy-sensitive workflows.

Clipboard history can contain passwords, payment details, health information, private messages, screenshots, and work documents. ClipNest is explicit about that risk because it is real.

1

Use Pause Capture before copying sensitive material.

2

Delete individual entries or clear history when you are done.

3

Only enable Google sync if you want synced history on that account.

4

Remember that ClipNest does not currently auto-exclude password managers or other sensitive apps.

Local storage locations

ClipNest stores data locally under your Windows local app data directory.

  • %LocalAppData%\ClipNest\history.json
  • %LocalAppData%\ClipNest\Images\
  • %LocalAppData%\ClipNest\settings.json
  • %LocalAppData%\ClipNest\Accounts\
