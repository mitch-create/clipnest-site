Privacy

Local only. Nothing leaves your PC.

ClipNest has no accounts, no cloud, and no server. Your history is a handful of files in your own Windows user folder.

What ClipNest never does

  • No accounts or sign-inThere is nothing to register for and nothing to log in to.
  • No cloud syncYour history stays on the PC where you copied it.
  • No uploadClipboard contents are never sent over the network.
  • No analytics or telemetryClipNest doesn't report how you use it, to anyone.

Where your history lives

Everything is stored under your local app data folder.

%LocalAppData%\ClipNest\ ├─ history.db # your history (SQLite) ├─ Images\ # saved images ├─ settings.json └─ error.log, crash.log, lifecycle.log

ClipNest doesn't encrypt these files, so other software running as your Windows account can read them. Logs record errors and app events, never clipboard contents. If you upgraded from an earlier version, the old history.json is kept as history.json.bak. When you uninstall, you choose whether to keep or delete these files.

ClipNest records everything you copy, unless you tell it not to.

That includes passwords from a password manager, one-time codes, and payment details. Add your password manager to Settings → Ignored Apps and its copies are never recorded. For anything else, turn on Pause Capture from the tray menu or Settings first, and delete the entry afterward if something slips through. Read the privacy policy

Release notes

What's new in 1.2.0

A faster way in, more control over what's kept, sturdier storage, and a simpler privacy story.

v1.2.0
  • New

    Global shortcut

    Press Ctrl+Shift+V anywhere to open or close ClipNest, or Ctrl+Alt+V if another app already has it. Pick any shortcut you like in Settings → Shortcut.

  • New

    Ignored apps

    Copies from apps you list in Settings → Ignored Apps, such as a password manager, are never recorded.

  • New

    History limits

    Choose how many clips to keep (100–2,000), a storage cap (50 MB–1 GB), and whether unpinned clips are deleted after 1, 7, 30, or 90 days. Pinned clips are always kept.

  • Improved

    Faster, sturdier storage

    History moved from one large JSON file to a small SQLite database that saves only what changed. Existing history is migrated automatically on first launch, and the old file is kept as history.json.bak.

  • Fixed

    Accurate “copied N×” counts

    Windows re-posts every copy through its own clipboard service. ClipNest used to count those re-posts as new copies, and they could slip past ignore rules. They are now recognized and skipped.

  • Fixed

    Images

    A browser's “Copy image” is saved as an image, transparency is kept when capturing and pasting, and re-copied images are saved immediately.

  • Improved

    Polish

    The panel remembers its size, list cards no longer zoom on hover (sharper text, smoother scrolling), and an unexpected error shows a tray notice instead of closing ClipNest.

  • Removed

    Cloud sync

    ClipNest is now local-only: no accounts, and no network access for your history.

  • Improved

    Installer

    Checks your Windows version and CPU architecture, closes a running ClipNest during upgrades, and removes the startup entry on uninstall.
