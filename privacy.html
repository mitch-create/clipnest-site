ClipNest Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

ClipNest stores clipboard history so you can search and reuse copied items later. Clipboard history can include sensitive information. This policy explains what ClipNest stores, how optional Google sync works, and what controls you have inside the app.

Clipboard history can contain sensitive data

ClipNest may store passwords, one-time codes, payment details, health information, work documents, screenshots, and private messages if they are copied to the clipboard.

ClipNest does not currently auto-exclude password managers or other sensitive apps. If you copy passwords, security codes, payment details, or regulated data, pause capture first and clear history afterward if needed.

What ClipNest stores

Depending on what you copy and which features you enable, ClipNest may store:

  • plain text
  • rich text / HTML
  • copied file lists
  • image thumbnails and saved image files
  • app settings
  • connected Google account metadata and protected refresh tokens used for sync

How local storage works

ClipNest stores clipboard history locally on your Windows device. Current local storage paths include:

  • %LocalAppData%\ClipNest\history.json
  • %LocalAppData%\ClipNest\Images\
  • %LocalAppData%\ClipNest\settings.json
  • %LocalAppData%\ClipNest\Accounts\

How Google sync works

If you choose to sign in with Google, ClipNest uses your Google Drive app data folder to sync eligible clipboard history between devices running ClipNest with the same Google account.

Google sync currently includes:

  • plain text
  • rich text / HTML
  • pinned state
  • image files used by synced entries

Copied file lists are device-specific and may remain local-only because those paths may not exist on another device.

Removing a Google account from ClipNest on one device removes the local saved account for that install. It does not automatically delete synced data already stored in that Google account's Drive app data folder.

What ClipNest does not do

ClipNest does not upload clipboard contents to a ClipNest-operated server.

Google sync is optional. You can use ClipNest locally without signing in.

Startup and system integration

If you enable Run on Startup, ClipNest uses the Windows startup mechanism available for your install type. Packaged MSIX installs request a Windows startup task. Classic installs use a per-user startup entry under HKCU\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Run.

Your controls

You can:

  • pause clipboard capture
  • delete individual entries
  • clear unpinned history or all history
  • remove a connected Google account from the device
  • turn off Run on Startup

Retention and deletion

Clipboard items remain in local history until you delete them, clear history, or ClipNest removes older unpinned items to stay within its local history and storage limits.

When Google sync is enabled, synced item records may remain in your Google account until they are removed by the app's sync process or you remove the associated app data from your Google account.
