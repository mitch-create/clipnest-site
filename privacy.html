Privacy policy

Your clipboard stays on your PC.

ClipNest is local-only. This policy explains what it stores, where it stores it, what it never does, and which controls are yours.

Clipboard history can be sensitive

ClipNest stores clipboard history so you can search and paste items later. That history can include passwords, one-time codes, payment details, health data, work documents, screenshots, and private messages.

ClipNest records everything you copy.

That includes text copied from password managers. Use Pause Capture (tray menu or Settings) before copying anything you don't want kept, and delete entries or clear history afterward if needed.

What ClipNest stores

ClipNest stores clipboard history on your device only. Depending on what you copy, it may store:

  • plain text
  • rich text and HTML
  • copied file lists
  • image thumbnails and saved image files
  • app settings

Where it's stored

ClipNest stores data under your local app data folder:

  • %LocalAppData%\ClipNest\history.json
  • %LocalAppData%\ClipNest\Images\
  • %LocalAppData%\ClipNest\settings.json
  • %LocalAppData%\ClipNest\error.log, crash.log, and lifecycle.log

Clipboard history, saved images, and local diagnostic logs are not encrypted by ClipNest. They are readable by software running as your Windows account. Diagnostic logs record errors and application lifecycle events, not clipboard contents.

What ClipNest does not do

ClipNest does not upload clipboard contents anywhere. Your history is never sent to ClipNest or to anyone else.

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No cloud sync
  • No upload
  • No analytics or telemetry

Startup and keyboard shortcut

If you enable Run on Startup, ClipNest uses the Windows startup mechanism available for your install type:

  • packaged MSIX installs request a Windows startup task
  • classic installs use a per-user startup entry under HKCU\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Run

ClipNest registers a global keyboard shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+V, or Ctrl+Alt+V if that is taken) to open its panel. Windows tells ClipNest only when that exact combination is pressed; ClipNest does not monitor or record your other keystrokes.

Your controls

You can:

  • pause capture
  • delete individual entries
  • clear unpinned items, images, duplicates, older items, or all history
  • turn off Run on Startup
  • choose whether to delete local history and settings during uninstall

Retention and deletion

Items stay in your local history until you delete them, clear history, or ClipNest removes the oldest unpinned items to stay within its limits of 500 entries and about 100 MB of unpinned history. Pinned items are kept.

When you uninstall, ClipNest asks whether to keep or permanently delete your history and settings. The default is to keep them.
