Common questions

Short answers to the issues most likely to come up during setup and daily use.

Why does sync take a few seconds?

ClipNest syncs through Google Drive app data, not a realtime push backend. Local capture happens first, then ClipNest syncs eligible changes in the background.

Why did a copied file list not appear on another PC?

File lists can be device-specific because the target paths may only exist on the machine where they were copied. Those entries may remain local-only.

Can I use ClipNest without Google?

Yes. Google sign-in is optional. ClipNest works as a local clipboard manager without any account.

What should I do before copying sensitive data?

Pause capture first. ClipNest does not currently auto-exclude password managers or other sensitive apps.

Where is the local data stored?

ClipNest stores history, settings, and related files under your Windows local app data folder.

What if Google sign-in fails?

Open ClipNest Settings and note the exact sync status text. Include that message in your support request.
