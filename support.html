Common questions

Quick answers

How do I open ClipNest?

Press Ctrl+Shift+V from any app. If another app already uses that shortcut, ClipNest uses Ctrl+Alt+V instead. You can pick a different shortcut in Settings → Shortcut, or click the ClipNest icon in the system tray.

The shortcut doesn't do anything.

Open Settings, or hover over the tray icon, to see which shortcut is active. If another app has taken it, choose Change under Settings → Shortcut and press a combination nothing else uses. You can always open ClipNest from the tray icon.

How do I keep passwords out of my history?

Add your password manager in Settings → Ignored Apps: pick it from the running apps or type its program name (for example KeePass.exe). Anything copied from an ignored app is never recorded.

For a one-off sensitive copy from another app, turn on Pause Capture in the tray menu (or Pause clipboard capture in Settings) first, then turn it off again. If something was already saved, delete that entry or clear history.

Does ClipNest sync between PCs or need an account?

No. Since version 1.2.0, ClipNest is local-only: no accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud sync. Each PC keeps its own history, and nothing is uploaded.

Windows SmartScreen warned me about the installer.

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say it comes from an unknown publisher. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

To confirm your download is intact, run Get-FileHash .\ClipNest_Setup.exe in PowerShell and compare the result with the checksum on the download section.

I closed the window. Is ClipNest still running?

Yes. Closing the panel keeps ClipNest running in the system tray so it can keep capturing. To stop it completely, right-click the tray icon and choose Exit.

How much history does ClipNest keep?

By default, up to 500 clips and about 100 MB. In Settings → History you can keep 100 to 2,000 clips, set a storage cap from 50 MB to 1 GB, and have unpinned clips deleted after 1, 7, 30, or 90 days.

When a limit is reached, the oldest unpinned clips are removed first. Pinned clips are never removed by these limits.

ClipNest showed a notice in the tray. What happened?

When something unexpected goes wrong, ClipNest shows a tray notice and keeps running instead of closing. If it keeps happening, email support with the notice text. error.log in %LocalAppData%\ClipNest\ has more detail and never contains clipboard contents.

Where does ClipNest store my data?

Under %LocalAppData%\ClipNest\ on your PC. History is kept in a local SQLite database, history.db. The privacy policy lists each file.

What happens to my history when I uninstall?

The uninstaller asks whether to keep or permanently delete your history and settings. The default is to keep them.
