Using ClipNest

ClipNest is a Windows clipboard manager provided for lawful use. You are responsible for content that you copy, store, or paste through the app.

No accounts or third-party services

ClipNest does not require an account and does not connect to any third-party service. It runs entirely on your PC.

Your data

Clipboard history can contain sensitive information. ClipNest records everything you copy, including text copied from password managers, unless you add that app to Ignored Apps. You decide what to copy, which apps to ignore, and when to pause capture, delete entries, or clear history.

Read the privacy policy for details about what ClipNest stores on your PC.
